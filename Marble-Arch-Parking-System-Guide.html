PART A — Management Overview

A1. Executive summary

The Marble Arch Hotel Nairobi Parking Management System runs the hotel's entire parking operation — from vehicle entry, bay allocation, ticketing and payment (cash, M-Pesa, card) through to shift handling, daily reconciliation, monthly subscriptions, complimentary parking and management reporting.

What it covers

  • Live control of 150 parking bays
  • Entry ticketing & exit billing with printed receipts
  • Monthly subscriptions & renewals
  • Complimentary parking (hotel guests, restaurant, staff, VIP…)
  • Cashier shifts with expected-vs-actual counts
  • End-of-day reconciliation with 3-stage approval
  • 17 management & financial reports

How it is used

  • Runs on desktop, laptop, tablet and mobile phones
  • Gate screens use large controls for fast barrier operation
  • Every figure comes from real database records — no demo data
  • Every payment and vehicle movement is auditable
  • Every privileged action is tied to a logged-in user & role

A7. Security & controls

  • Passwords are hashed; sessions expire; logins are throttled; no technical errors leak to users.
  • Payments cannot be edited or deleted — corrections go through the refund approval process.
  • Audit logs cannot be edited or deleted and capture user, role, action, before/after values.
  • Finalised reconciliation days are locked against modification.
  • No secrets (M-Pesa keys, service keys, passwords) exist in the web interface code.
  • Production checklist: restore login (NO_LOGIN=false), change all default passwords, set a long JWT_SECRET, serve over HTTPS.

PART B — Staff User Guide

B1. Getting started (all staff)

  1. Open the Parking Website preview. The system opens straight into the Dashboard (no login screen at present).
  2. Use the left menu to move between sections — you only see what your role allows.
  3. Top bar essentials:
    • 🔍 Search — type a registration, ticket, customer, phone or payment reference.
    • 🅿️ Bay chip — free bays at a glance.
    • ⏰ Shift chip — shows your open shift (No shift means open one before collecting money).
    • 🔔 Bell — notifications (expiries, variances, approvals).
  4. On a phone, use the bottom bar: Entry · Exit · Bays · Cars for one-tap gate work.
Golden rule: no open shift = no money collection. The system blocks payments until you open a shift.

B2. Parking Attendant guide

Register a vehicle entry

  1. Go to Vehicle Entry.
  2. Type the registration (e.g. KAA 123A) — the system warns you if the car is already parked or has an active monthly cover.
  3. Select vehicle type, driver name and phone.
  4. Choose the category (Daily Customer for normal paid parking).
  5. Leave bay on Auto-assign (recommended) or pick a free bay.
  6. Press 🎫 Issue ticket, then Print and hand the ticket to the driver.

Complimentary vehicles (hotel guest, restaurant, staff, VIP…)

  1. Choose the matching complimentary category.
  2. Fill in comp category, who authorised it (e.g. Duty Manager) and the reason/reference.
  3. Only supervisors/managers can authorise — attendants will see a NOT AUTHORIZED warning and must call one.

Live bays & moving a car

  • Live Parking shows every bay: available occupied complimentary monthly reserved out of service.
  • Tap a bay to see the vehicle, ticket and entry time; use Move to relocate it (every move is logged).

B3. Cashier guide

1. Open your shift

  1. Go to Shifts → Open shift, enter your station and opening cash float.
  2. The top bar chip changes to your shift number (e.g. #12). All money you collect is now linked to it.

2. Process a vehicle exit

  1. Go to Vehicle Exit, type the registration, ticket or phone and press Find.
  2. Check the bill: entry time, duration, fee, any discount, amounts already paid and Balance due.
  3. Collect payment on the correct tab:
    • 💵 Cash — enter amount, record.
    • 📱 M-Pesa — enter amount + the transaction code from the customer's SMS (required). Recorded as manual until the M-Pesa API is connected — always verify the SMS.
    • 💳 Card — enter amount + authorisation/reference number from the PDQ terminal (required), plus terminal ID.
  4. When balance is zero, press ✅ Complete exit — the barrier copy (receipt) prints and the bay is freed automatically.

3. Discounts

  • Cashiers/attendants request discounts (promotion, fixed amount, percentage or free) with a reason.
  • A pending discount blocks exit until a manager approves or rejects it (in Exit or Promotions).

4. Close your shift

  1. Go to Shifts → Close shift. The system shows what it expects per method.
  2. Count and enter your actual cash, M-Pesa and card totals.
  3. If anything differs, you must write an explanation — control is notified automatically.

B4. Supervisor guide

  • Duplicate / “already parked”: at Entry, tick Supervisor override to admit the vehicle anyway (audited).
  • Backdate entry: with override ticked, set the true entry time (e.g. vehicle admitted during a network outage).
  • Wrong entry: open the session (Parking or Live view) → Void with a reason. Sessions with payments cannot be voided — use refunds.
  • Reserved bays: assignable to monthly-reserved vehicles, VIP/management complimentary, or by override.
  • Complimentary: record the authoriser and reason at entry; review the register under Promotions → Complimentary.
  • Shift review: open any closed shift and mark it reviewed.
  • Reconciliation: perform the supervisor review step on the day's figures.

B5. Manager / Controller / Accounts guide

End-of-day reconciliation

  1. Open Reconciliation and pick the date. Figures are computed live from transactions.
  2. Save draft (controller), then reviews in order: Supervisor → Controller → Manager finalize.
  3. Once finalized, the day is locked — it cannot be edited.

Money oversight

  • Payments → Refunds: approve/reject requests, then mark approved ones paid.
  • Promotions → Discounts: approve/reject pending discount requests.
  • Reports → Variance: every non-zero shift variance with its explanation.
  • Audit Logs: who did what, when, with before/after values.

B6. Monthly parking how-to

  1. Open Monthly Parking → New subscription (you need an open shift — the fee is recorded against it).
  2. Enter customer, company, phone, registration, package and payment reference. Expiry auto-calculates (30 days).
  3. At entry, covered vehicles are detected automatically and park under the monthly category — no daily charge.
  4. Renew from the subscription record (continues the day after expiry). Suspend misused passes; expired passes are rejected at entry automatically.
  5. Watch days-remaining badges and dashboard/bell alerts for expiries within 7 days.

B7. Reports & exports how-to

  1. Open Reports, pick one of the 17 reports on the left.
  2. Set From / To dates (plus user, method or search where offered) and press Run.
  3. Press ⬇ CSV to download for Excel, or 🖨 Print for a hotel-letterhead printout/PDF.
Financial reports (revenue, cash, M-Pesa, card, variance, discounts, refunds) are visible only to roles with financial permission.

B8. Offline & pending sync

  • If the network drops mid-operation, entries, exits and payments are saved to a local outbox instead of being lost.
  • They always show as Pending sync — never as stored records.
  • When back online, tap the 📤 N pending sync chip in the top bar to send them to the server.

B9. Troubleshooting & FAQ

Message / situationWhat to do
“Already parked” / duplicate sessionThe car is inside on another ticket. Call a supervisor to override if it is genuinely a second entry.
“Open a shift before collecting payments”Go to Shifts and open your shift first.
Exit blocked — discount awaiting approvalA manager must approve/reject it (Exit screen or Promotions).
“Outstanding balance…” at exitCollect the full balance first; partial payments are kept on the ticket.
“Variance requires an explanation”Counted totals differ from expected — write the reason to close the shift.
M-Pesa / card reference requiredType the SMS transaction code (M-Pesa) or PDQ auth code (card) — they are mandatory.
Reserved bay refusedReserved bays need a monthly-reserved or authorised vehicle, or supervisor override.
Monthly pass rejected at entryIt is expired, suspended or not yet started — renew it under Monthly Parking.
Day is finalized / cannot editCorrect — finalised days are locked. Raise any correction with management for the next open day.
“No parking bays available”The lot is full — admit only with supervisor override (no bay assigned).

B10. Gate checklists

🚗 Entry

  • ☐ Registration typed correctly
  • ☐ Right category (paid vs complimentary)
  • ☐ Complimentary: authoriser + reason filled
  • ☐ Ticket printed & handed over

🏁 Exit

  • ☐ Correct vehicle found (reg/ticket/phone)
  • ☐ Bill checked (duration, fee, discounts)
  • ☐ Payment recorded on the right method + reference
  • ☐ Balance zero → Complete exit → receipt printed

⏰ Shift end

  • ☐ Takings counted per method ☐ Actuals entered ☐ Variance explained (if any) ☐ Shift closed

B7 · PDF reports & phone sharing

Every report (all 17) and the Daily Reconciliation now carry PDF and Share buttons.

  • PDF – downloads a letterheaded PDF (hotel logo, name, contacts, footer) of the current report.
  • Share on a phone – opens the phone share sheet so the actual PDF file can be sent straight to WhatsApp or Email.
  • Share on a computer – shows the PDF in a preview pane with: download, print / save-as-PDF, WhatsApp / Email summary links, one-tap copy of the summary, and a text box for manual copying.

PDFs carry the same figures as the on-screen report — cash, M-Pesa (verified/manual split), card, refunds, net revenue, vehicle counts, and approval names where applicable.
